Add an app to your course

One course file puts an app into your LMS course: a module for each week or unit with every graded assignment, its points and a link to your copy of the app.

  • Canvas
  • Schoology
  • Moodle
  • Brightspace

1Pick the app

2Paste your student link

3Choose the assignments

Just the first part is ticked: add assignments as you go. Come back for the next week when you start it (the same link and names each time), or tick everything to set up the whole unit now. They arrive unpublished.

4Download and import it

Made in your browser. Your link and the file never leave your computer.

Downloaded . Now import it into your course:
Canvas
  1. In your course, open Settings ▸ Import Course Content.
  2. Content Type: Common Cartridge 1.x Package. Choose the file.
  3. Pick All content, then Import. When it says Completed, the weeks are in Modules.
Schoology, Moodle, Brightspace and others

Look for Import and choose Common Cartridge (also called IMS CC or .imscc). You get the modules, the link to your copy and each assignment’s instructions. Whether they arrive as graded assignments with points depends on your LMS; if not, add the gradebook columns yourself, using the names and points listed above.

Good to know

  • Keep the names. In Canvas, the app’s Push finds each assignment by its exact name and sends scores and comments to it, so you never get a second column. If you rename one in Canvas, Push makes a new one.
  • Points match the app. Push keeps them in step if you leave something out for the whole class later.
  • Not on Canvas? Enter scores from the app’s gradebook. The app works the same either way.
  • On Canvas, you may not need more than the first file. When you open a week and Push it (or save it in Due dates & Canvas…), the app makes that week’s assignments itself, with the same names. Don’t import a week the app has already made: you’d get a second, empty column with the same name.
  • Teaching two sections? Import once per course. Two app classes can share one Canvas course.
